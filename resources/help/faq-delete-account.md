---
title: How do I delete my account?
category: faq
platforms: [web, mobile]
order: 40
---

In the app: go to **••• → Account → Delete Account**. We'll email you a
confirmation link; once confirmed, your account and associated data are
permanently deleted. You can also email
[support@tts.band](mailto:support@tts.band) and we'll delete your account
within 30 days.
