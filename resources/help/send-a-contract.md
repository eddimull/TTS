Turn agreed terms into a signed contract without leaving the booking.

## Set terms
Open the booking's **Contract** tab and fill in the terms — fee, date,
venue, and whatever else your band's contract covers. Review everything
before sending; once it's out for signature, changing terms means an
amendment.

## Send for e-signature
Send the contract to the client from the same tab. They receive a link into
their client portal where they can review and sign electronically — no
printing, scanning, or emailing PDFs back and forth.

## Amendments
If terms need to change after a contract has gone out — a date shift, a fee
adjustment — you don't edit the signed document directly. Sending an
amendment voids the original envelope and resets it for signing, so both
sides sign off on a clean, current version rather than a patched one.

## History
The Contract tab keeps a history of every version sent, when it was signed,
and any amendments along the way, so you always know which terms are
currently in force.
