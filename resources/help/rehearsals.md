Rehearsals get their own schedule, separate from gigs, so practice time
doesn't get lost in the booking calendar.

## Recurring schedules
Set up a rehearsal to repeat weekly, biweekly, or on whatever cadence your
band practices, instead of creating each one by hand.

## Attendance
Band members RSVP to each rehearsal, so you know who's showing up before you
walk in. Attendance is tracked per rehearsal, giving you a record of who's
been making it to practice.

## Cancel and restore
Plans change — a rehearsal can be cancelled without deleting it. Cancelled
rehearsals show up crossed out on the calendar so nobody drives out for a
practice that isn't happening, and a cancelled rehearsal can be restored if
plans change again.

## AI rehearsal planner
On the app, open a rehearsal and use the planner to generate a practice plan
based on your upcoming setlists — songs that need work, new material coming
up. You can save the generated plan straight to the rehearsal's notes so the
whole band sees it.

## Inviting subs
If a regular member can't make a rehearsal, you can invite a substitute
player just for that session, the same way you would for a gig.
