You're the band owner — here's the path from empty band to first gig.

## 1. Set up the band
Head to **Band Settings** (web: your name menu → Bands; app: ••• tab → Band Settings)
and add your band's name, logo, and Stripe account if you want to take payments
through the client portal.

## 2. Invite your musicians
Open **Personnel** and invite members by email, or share your invite QR code —
anyone who scans it lands straight in your band. Members see events, setlists,
charts, and chat; you control per-area permissions from Band Settings.

## 3. Create your first booking
A **booking** tracks a gig from inquiry to payout: venue, contacts, contract,
payments, lineup, and the events that make up the night. Start one from
**Bookings → New**, then send the contract for e-signature when terms are set.

## 4. Run the night
Build a **setlist** for the event, and when downbeat comes, start a **live
session** — every bandmate's phone follows the current song in real time.
