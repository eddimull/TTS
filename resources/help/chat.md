Chat (mobile only, under the **Messages** tab) is how your band talks to
each other without leaving the app.

## Direct messages and group threads
Message a single bandmate directly, or use group threads for the whole band
or a subset of it — useful for coordinating a specific gig's lineup without
pinging everyone.

## Reactions
React to a message instead of sending a reply when a quick thumbs-up or
laugh is all that's needed.

## Delivered and seen
Messages show delivered and seen status, so you know whether the rest of the
band has actually read that "call time moved up" message before showtime.

## Comment threads
Separate from chat, events and bookings each have their own comment thread
attached to them — discussion that's specifically about that gig or that
date stays there instead of getting buried in a general chat.

## Push notifications
New messages and comments trigger a push notification, so you don't have to
keep the app open to stay in the loop. Notifications are per-conversation, so
tapping one takes you straight to the relevant thread.
