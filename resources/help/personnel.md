Personnel is where you manage who's in your band and what they can do.

## Members and permissions
Every band member has an account with permissions the band owner controls —
who can see finances, edit bookings, manage personnel, and so on. Not
everyone needs full access to everything; a sub or a part-time player can be
scoped down to just what they need.

## Rosters
A roster is a saved group of members — your usual lineup for a certain kind
of gig, say. Rosters aren't just a list: assigning a roster to future events
keeps them in sync, so adding or removing someone from the roster updates
every upcoming event that uses it automatically, instead of editing each
event's lineup by hand.

## Substitute invites
When a regular player can't make a gig or rehearsal, invite a substitute
just for that date. Subs get access to what they need for that specific
event — setlist, notes, chat — without becoming full band members.

On the web, manage all of this from your band's settings (your name menu →
Bands → your band). On the app, it's in the Operations hub under Personnel
(band owners only).
