The Finances section (web only) is where you track the money side of the
band, across all your bookings.

## Overview & revenue
The overview gives you a snapshot of how the band is doing — revenue coming
in and what's still outstanding. The Revenue view breaks that down further so
you can see trends over time.

## Paid and unpaid
Paid/Unpaid and Unpaid/Paid Services show which bookings have been settled
and which are still waiting on a payment, so you can follow up before a gig
sneaks by unpaid.

## Invoices
Generate and send invoices straight from a booking's Finances tab. Clients
pay through the client portal, and the payment shows up here once it clears.

## Payout calculator
Work out how a gig's fee splits across band members before you commit to a
structure — useful for one-off arrangements or sanity-checking a new split.

## Payout flow configurations
For a repeatable split, set up a payout configuration once — a node-based
flow you build to define how money moves from the gig fee to each member's
share — and apply it to future bookings instead of calculating it every time.
