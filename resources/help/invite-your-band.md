Get your bandmates into the app so they can see gigs, setlists, and chat
alongside you.

## Invite by email
On the web, open your band's settings (your name menu → Bands → your band)
and invite someone by email. On the app, use Operations hub → Personnel
(owners only). Either way, the invitee gets a link to create an account and
land straight in your band.

## Invite by QR code
The same screen shows an invite QR code. If the person scanning already has
Bandmate installed, their camera opens straight to the join screen for your
band. If they don't have it installed yet, the scan takes them to install
the app first — once it's installed, they scan the code again (or use the
link) to finish joining. Joining is also possible from the web without
installing anything.

## What invitees see
Once someone joins, they land on the dashboard with your band's upcoming
events already visible. What else they can see and do — finances, personnel,
editing bookings — depends on the permissions you set for them; new members
start with a standard member role that you can adjust from your band's
settings at any time.
