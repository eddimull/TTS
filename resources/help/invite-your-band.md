Get your bandmates into the app so they can see gigs, setlists, and chat
alongside you.

## Invite by email
Open **Personnel** (web: Personnel in the nav; app: Operations hub →
Personnel, owners only) and invite someone by email. They'll get a link to
create an account and land straight in your band.

## Invite by QR code
Personnel also shows an invite QR code. Anyone who scans it with their
phone's camera is deep-linked straight into the app — if they already have
Bandmate installed it opens directly to the join screen for your band; if
not, it takes them to install it first, then finishes the join once they
open the app.

## What invitees see
Once someone joins, they land on the dashboard with your band's upcoming
events already visible. What else they can see and do — finances, personnel,
editing bookings — depends on the permissions you set for them; new members
start with a standard member role that you can adjust from Personnel at any
time.
