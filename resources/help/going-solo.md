Bandmate works for solo acts too — you're just a band of one.

Create a band with your act's name. You get the same toolkit: bookings with
contracts and client payments, a repertoire and setlists, a media library, and
stats on every gig you play.

As a solo act you'll mostly live in **Bookings** (gigs, contracts, getting paid)
and the **Dashboard** (your calendar). If you later add players — a duo gig, a
dep drummer — invite them from **Personnel** and they'll see just the events
they're on.
