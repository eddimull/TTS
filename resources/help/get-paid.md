Collect payment from a client without leaving Bandmate or chasing a check.

## Set up Stripe
Before you can take payments, connect a Stripe account from your band
settings. This is what lets clients pay you directly through their client
portal — Bandmate doesn't hold or move funds itself.

## Invoice the client
From a booking's Finances tab, create an invoice for the amount due — a
deposit, a balance, or the full fee. Send it to the client and it appears in
their client portal.

## The client pays
The client opens their portal, reviews the invoice, and pays through Stripe
right there. No separate app or account is needed on their end beyond the
portal link you've already sent them.

## Payment history
Once paid, the invoice updates automatically and the payment shows up in the
booking's Finances tab and in the client's own payment history in their
portal — so both sides have a clear, matching record without you manually
marking anything as paid.
