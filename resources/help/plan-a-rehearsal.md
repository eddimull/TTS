Let the app draft a rehearsal plan for you instead of staring at a blank
notes field.

## Open the planner
From a rehearsal, open the AI planner. It looks at your band's upcoming
setlists — the songs you're about to need for the next few gigs — and
generates a suggested plan for what to work on.

## What it generates
The plan focuses on songs that are coming up soon and could use practice:
new additions to the repertoire, songs that haven't been played in a while,
or anything flagged as needing work. It's a starting point, not a rigid
script — treat it as a head start on deciding what to run.

## Save it to notes
If the generated plan looks good, save it straight to the rehearsal's notes
so the whole band sees the same agenda when they check the rehearsal. You
can edit it before or after saving — add a song, drop one, reorder — it's
your rehearsal, the planner just gets you moving faster.
