The dashboard is your band's calendar — every gig, rehearsal, and event in one
scrolling list, soonest first.

## What you see
Each entry shows the date, a short title, and the type of event (gig,
rehearsal, or general event). Tap or click any entry to open its full detail:
time, venue, attire, lineup, and notes.

## Loading more
The dashboard only loads a window of events at a time so it stays fast. Scroll
to the bottom to load older or newer entries — you don't need to jump to a
separate archive to see what happened last month or what's coming next
quarter.

## On the app
Pull down on the list to refresh it — handy right before you walk into a gig
to make sure nothing changed last minute. Tap the month header at the top to
open a picker and jump straight to any month, instead of scrolling one event
at a time.

## On the web
The web dashboard shows the same calendar with more room to scan a busy week
at a glance, and links straight into Bookings, Finances, and the rest of your
band's tools from the side navigation.
