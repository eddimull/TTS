Keep your band's gigs and rehearsals visible in the calendar app you already
use, alongside everything else in your life.

## On the app
Go to **••• → Add to Calendar**. You'll get a feed URL (an ICS link) that you
can add to your phone's calendar app as a subscribed calendar — new and
changed events keep showing up automatically without you re-adding anything.
If the feed ever gets out of sync or you want a fresh link, use the reset
button to generate a new URL and re-add it to your calendar app.

## On the web
Connect Google Calendar from your band settings. Once connected, your band's
events sync into your Google Calendar the same way, so they show up next to
your personal appointments without manual entry.

## Why bother
Once it's connected, you don't have to keep opening Bandmate just to check
what's next — your regular calendar app already has it, with reminders and
all.
