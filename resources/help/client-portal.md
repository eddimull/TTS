Your clients don't get a full Bandmate account — they get their own portal,
scoped to just their booking, with a separate login.

## What clients see
- **Contract** — the terms you sent, and a place to sign electronically.
- **Invoices** — what's owed and when it's due.
- **Payments** — clients pay directly through the portal via Stripe, and can
  see their payment history for the booking.
- **Media** — any photos or files you've shared with them, like event photos
  or a signed rider.
- **Questionnaire responses** — any questionnaire you've sent shows up here
  for them to fill out, and they can review what they submitted.

## Why it matters
The portal keeps clients out of your band's internal tools (finances across
other gigs, chat, other clients) while still giving them everything they
need for their own event — no emailing PDFs back and forth or chasing a
signature by phone.

Clients receive a link to their portal when you send them a contract or
invoice; they don't need to be invited to your band to use it.
