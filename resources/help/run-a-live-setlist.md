Take your setlist from a plan on paper (well, on screen) to something the
whole band follows in real time on stage.

## Build the setlist
Open the event and add songs from your repertoire to the setlist, arranging
them in the order you'll play. Group into sets if the night has breaks, and
leave notes on any song that needs a reminder.

## Start the live session
When it's showtime, the band captain opens the setlist and starts a live
session. Every bandmate with the app open automatically sees the session
begin.

## Advance songs
As the captain taps to move to the next song, every follower's screen
updates in real time to show the current song and what's coming up next — no
one has to ask what's next mid-set. Only the captain can advance the set or
end the session; followers just watch it update.

## Follower view
If you're not the captain, you're a follower: your screen shows the current
song highlighted with the next one queued below it, and you can submit a
quick crowd reaction without disrupting the flow.

## End the session
The captain ends the session once the set wraps. The setlist stays attached
to the event afterward as a record of what you played.
