An event is a single date and time — a performance, a load-in, a sound
check. Bookings can include several events; open one from your dashboard or
from the booking's Events tab.

## Event detail
Every event page shows the time, venue, attire for the gig, the lineup
(who's playing), notes from the band or the client, and any attachments —
stage plots, parking instructions, whatever the gig needs on hand.

## Advance sheet
On the web, each event can generate an advance sheet — a single printable
summary of the load-in time, venue details, contacts, and logistics, handy to
send ahead or hand to the crew.

## Substitutes
If someone on the lineup can't make it, a substitute can be assigned to their
spot. The sub sees the event just like a regular band member — setlist,
notes, attachments — without needing full access to the rest of the band.

## Comments
Each event has its own comment thread, separate from the booking's chat, so
discussion about that specific date — timing changes, a gear question — stays
attached to the event it's about.
