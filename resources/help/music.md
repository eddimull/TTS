Your band's repertoire lives in two connected places: a song list and a
charts library.

## Song list
The song list is your repertoire — every song your band knows or is
learning, independent of any particular gig. This is what setlists are built
from.

## Charts library
Charts are the sheet music itself, stored as PDFs. Upload charts to the
library so every band member can pull up the same part on their phone or
laptop instead of hunting for a paper copy.

## Linking songs to charts
A song in your repertoire can be linked to its chart, so opening the song
from a setlist takes you straight to the sheet music for it — no separate
search through the charts library mid-rehearsal.

On the app, find both under **Library**. On the web, they live under Assets
as Sheet Music and Song List.
