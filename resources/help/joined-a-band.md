Welcome aboard — your account is linked to the band, and here's where everything lives.

## Your dashboard
The **Dashboard** is your calendar: every gig, rehearsal, and event your band
has scheduled, newest first. Pull down to refresh on the app; tap any event for
the full details — time, venue, attire, lineup, and notes.

## Know the night
Each event page has the **setlist**, your **charts** (sheet music), attachments,
and a comment thread. During the show, the live setlist follows along on your
phone as the captain advances songs.

## Rehearsals
See scheduled rehearsals, RSVP your attendance, and read the plan. If a
rehearsal is cancelled you'll see it crossed out — no phantom drives to the studio.

## Get paid
**My Stats** shows your personal gig history, pay, and mileage. Payouts are
configured by the band owner; your share shows up per booking.
