A booking is one gig, tracked from the first inquiry all the way to getting
paid. It's the hub that ties together everything about that job.

## Tabs on a booking
- **Events** — the individual dates and times that make up the booking (load-in,
  performance, sometimes a rehearsal tied to the gig).
- **Contacts** — the client and any other people you're coordinating with.
- **Contract** — terms, e-signature status, and amendment history.
- **Finances** — what's owed, what's paid, and invoices sent to the client.
- **Lineup** — who's playing, including any substitutes.
- **Payout** — how the money splits across the band once the gig is paid.
- **History** — a timeline of changes: contract sent, signed, payments
  received, and so on.

## Statuses
A booking moves through a lifecycle as it progresses — from an initial
inquiry, through contract and payment, to a completed and paid gig. The
status badge on the booking list tells you at a glance where each one stands,
so you can spot the ones still waiting on a signature or a deposit.

## Where to start
Create a booking from **Bookings → New** on the web, or from the Operations
hub on the app. Fill in the venue and date, then work through the tabs as
details firm up — you don't have to complete everything at once.
