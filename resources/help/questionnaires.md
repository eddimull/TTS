Questionnaires let you collect information from a client before the gig —
song requests, dietary needs, ceremony details, whatever your band needs to
know ahead of time.

## Building a questionnaire
Create a questionnaire once, with whatever questions you want to ask, and
reuse it across bookings instead of building a new form for every client.

## Sending per booking
Attach a questionnaire to a specific booking and send it to the client. It
shows up in their client portal, where they can fill it out on their own
time.

## Reading responses
Once a client submits, their answers appear on the booking so the whole band
can see them — no forwarding a separate email around before the gig. You can
come back to a booking's responses any time to double-check a detail before
you play.

Find Questionnaires in the Assets section on the web, or in the Operations
hub on the app. Both platforms show the same responses, so whoever checks in
before the gig has the full picture.
