Every event can have its own setlist — the order of songs you're playing
that night.

## Building a setlist
Add songs from your band's repertoire and drag them into the order you want
to play. You can group songs into sets and leave notes on individual songs
(key changes, count-offs, whatever the band needs to remember).

## AI generation
On the web, you can generate a setlist starting point with AI — it suggests
an order based on your repertoire and past setlists, which you can then
reorder, swap out, or trim before the show.

## Running a live session
When it's time to play, the band captain starts a live session from the
setlist screen. Every band member with the app open sees the current song
and what's coming up next, and their screen advances automatically as the
captain moves through the set — nobody has to ask "what are we playing
next?" mid-song. Only the captain can advance songs or end the session.

## Crowd reactions
During a live session, band members following along can submit quick
reactions to the current song — a lightweight pulse-check on what's landing,
visible in real time without interrupting the show.
