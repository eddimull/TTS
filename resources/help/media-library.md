The media library holds your band's photos and files — everything from
promo shots to a signed rider.

## Band-wide and per-booking
Some media belongs to the band as a whole (press photos, logos); some
belongs to a specific booking (photos from that night, a venue's stage
plot). Open the library from a booking to see just that gig's files, or from
the Operations hub / Assets menu to browse everything.

## Folders
Organize files into folders so the library stays navigable as it grows —
group by event, by type, or however your band works.

## Sharing
Files can be shared with people outside the band — send a client their event
photos, or hand a folder to a photographer without giving them access to the
rest of your band's data.

## Google Drive
On the web, you can connect a Google Drive account to the media library from
band settings, so files can be pulled in from or pushed out to Drive instead
of living only inside Bandmate.
